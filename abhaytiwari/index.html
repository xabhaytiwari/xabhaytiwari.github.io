I build reinforcement-learning agents that come with mathematical guarantees.

I'm an AI Scientist at DataPOEM and an undergraduate in AI and Data Science. Until July 2026 I did research on spatio-temporal deep learning at IIT Hyderabad.

Most of machine learning guesses patterns from static pictures. I'm after something harder: how an agent works out what to do when every action changes its future, and how to prove it will. My work spans the mathematics of learning algorithms and the engineering that makes them run.

  • What must an agent remember?A map with every blade of grass is as useless as the world. What is the smallest state an agent needs to act well, and can it learn that abstraction instead of being handed it?
  • Safety by constructionPenalties punish a failure after it happens. Can conservation laws and constraints live inside the policy itself, so certain failures cannot occur?
  • Credit across timeA quiet pawn on move 12 wins the game on move 62. Can an agent trace a win to its true cause without hand-built reward breadcrumbs?
  • Structure versus scaleWhen does known physics replace millions of samples, and can we prove how many it saves?

My preprint asks whether smarter data selection beats random sampling. Under matched compute, it often doesn't.
